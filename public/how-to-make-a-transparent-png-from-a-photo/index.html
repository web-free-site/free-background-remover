How to make a transparent PNG from a photo

A transparent PNG is useful for logos, product shots, and portraits that need to sit on a new background. Free Background Remover can drop the existing background and export a PNG you can reuse in slides, documents, or a shop listing.

Steps

  1. Choose a photo with a clear subject. A single subject on a contrasting background usually cuts more cleanly than a group or a low-contrast shot.
  2. Remove the background in the browser. Add the file to Free Background Remover and run the cutout on your device.
  3. Download a PNG and inspect it. Open the PNG in a viewer that shows transparency (often as a checkerboard). Look for holes in the subject or leftover background.
  4. Place it on the new background. Import the PNG into your design or document. Do not flatten it until you are happy with the result.

File format

JPEG cannot store transparency. If you need a clear background, keep the export as PNG (or another format that supports an alpha channel).

Copyright and people

Only edit photos you have the right to use. Be careful when publishing pictures of people who have not agreed to that use.

How to remove a photo background for a CV

Some CV templates and job sites look cleaner with a portrait on a plain or transparent background. You can cut the background out in the browser with Free Background Remover, then place the result on a white or brand-colored canvas.

Steps

  1. Start from a clear head-and-shoulders photo. Use even lighting and a simple original background if you can. Busy scenes and hair against a similar color are harder to cut cleanly.
  2. Run the background remover. Open Free Background Remover, add the photo, and wait for the cutout. The model runs in your browser; the image is not uploaded to our servers.
  3. Check the edges. Zoom in on hair, glasses, and shoulders. If a piece of the background remains, try a different source photo rather than publishing a messy edge.
  4. Export and place it on your CV. Download a transparent PNG and add it to your CV template, or to <a href="https://free-cv-maker.web-free.site/">Free CV Maker</a> if you are building the document there. Keep the portrait small and professional.

Follow the employer's rules

Some applications forbid photos entirely. Others require a specific background. Do not add a portrait if the advert says not to.

Limits of an automatic cutout

On-device models can miss fine hair or semi-transparent edges. This tool is a convenience, not a studio retouching service.
